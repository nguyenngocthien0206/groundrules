# DR-008: Cost of goods sold uses the store's weighted average cost

## Context
Gross margin needs the cost of what was sold. The shelf sells the lot that
expires first, but neither POS records which batch a sale came from, so
the true cost of a line is unknown. Batches of the same SKU cost
different amounts: suppliers change prices, short-dated lots come
discounted, and a transfer from another store carries that store's cost.

## Decision
The cost of one base unit is the **weighted average over every receipt of
that SKU at that store**: total value received divided by total base units
received, from suppliers and from other stores, opening stock included
(`int_store_sku_costs`). Each sales line costs `qty_base` times that
figure (`fct_sales.cogs_excl_vat`). `gross_margin` = `net_revenue` minus
the sum of that cost. Returned goods are destroyed, so their cost is
**not** reversed.

## Options considered
- **Weighted average per store and SKU (chosen):** a method Vietnamese
  accounting standards allow and many accounting teams already use, and it
  reflects what each store actually paid, including stock it received by
  transfer.
- **Weighted average per SKU for the whole chain:** one cost per SKU is
  simpler, but a store that buys at a worse price or takes discounted
  short-dated lots would show someone else's margin.
- **First expiry, first out (FEFO), like the shelf:** the most accurate in
  theory, but impossible without the batch on the sales line.

## Consequences
- Margin by store is comparable and explainable from the store's receipts.
- The average uses the whole extract, so a new receipt at a different
  price changes the cost of earlier sales on the next run. For monthly
  close, Finance freezes the cost per month; this example does not.
- Returns lower revenue but not cost, so heavy returns show up as lower
  margin. The Head of Supply Chain wanted that visible.

## Worked example
Store NW004, SKU 100105 "Vitamin D3 1000IU (Box 10x10)" (base unit:
tablet), three receipts:

| Received | Source | Tablets | Cost per tablet | Value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2025-11-14 | purchase order | 500 | 665.19 | 332,595 |
| 2026-08-18 | transfer | 100 | 617.45 | 61,745 |
| 2026-09-10 | purchase order | 500 | 624.53 | 312,265 |

- Weighted average = (332,595 + 61,745 + 312,265) / (500 + 100 + 500)
  = 706,605 / 1,100 = **642.37 VND per tablet** (642.3682 unrounded).
- A sale of one strip (10 tablets) at NW004 costs 10 x 642.3682 = 6,423.68 VND.
