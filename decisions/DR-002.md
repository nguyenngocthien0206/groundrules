# DR-002: Returns count in the month they come back

## Context
Returns arrive days or weeks after the sale. Of the returns that can be
matched to their sale, about three in ten land in a later month. Finance and Sales each had a dashboard, and they disagreed
every month by exactly the returns that crossed a month boundary.

Finance closes each month and reports it to the board. If a July sale is
returned on 18 August and the refund is booked against July, July's
closed number changes after it was reported. Sales sees it the other way:
store targets are monthly, and a manager whose July sale is refunded in
August loses August revenue for something July did.

## Decision
`net_revenue` subtracts refunds (before VAT, DR-006) on the **return date**
(`returns_excl_vat`, agg time `return_date`, in
`dbt/models/semantic/fct_returns.yml`), whatever month the sale was in. A
return with no matching sale still counts, on its return date.

## Options considered
- **Return month (chosen):** a closed month never changes; the refund is
  recorded when the cash leaves. Matches how the books are kept.
- **Sale month:** fairer for monthly store targets, but every closed month
  keeps moving for up to two months, and returns with no receipt cannot be
  placed at all. Asked for by the Head of Sales.
- **Both, side by side as certified metrics:** two certified revenue
  numbers is the problem this project exists to fix.

The CFO broke the tie at the monthly business review. Sales keeps an
uncertified sale-month view for incentive calculations only; it is not
offered to agents and not called revenue.

## Consequences
- A month's net revenue is final once the month is over.
- Store managers see refunds of last month's sales in this month. The
  Head of Sales accepted this with the incentive view as a workaround.
- `fct_returns` keeps both `return_date` and `sale_business_date`, so the
  sale-month view stays possible without a second certified metric.

## Worked example
Store NW001, return RT0003323: one box of SKU 100096 "Simethicone 80mg
(Box 3x10)", sold on 2026-07-23 (receipt A0012607230024), returned on
2026-08-18. Refund 42,000 VND with VAT, 40,000 VND before VAT (5%).
It lowers **August**, not July.

NW001 for August 2026:
- Sales after discounts, before VAT, sold in August: 238,404,424.24 VND.
- Refunds before VAT, returned in August: 2,100,467.53 VND.
- `net_revenue` = 238,404,424.24 - 2,100,467.53 = **236,303,956.71 VND**.
- Counting refunds by sale month instead would subtract 2,007,471.86 and
  give 236,396,952.38: the two dashboards differed by 92,995.67 VND for
  this one store.
