# DR-009: Sell-through divides units sold by units received net of transfers out

## Context
Sell-through compares what a store sold with what it received. Inside a
chain, "received" has three readings: deliveries from suppliers only,
everything that arrived (suppliers and other stores), or everything that
arrived minus what the store sent on to other stores.

Each reading is right somewhere and wrong somewhere else. Supplier-only
makes a store that lives on transfers look like it sells more than it
gets. Counting transfers in without transfers out counts the same tablet
twice for the chain: once when the supplier delivered it, once when it
moved between stores.

## Decision
`sell_through_rate` = base units sold / (base units received from
suppliers and other stores - base units transferred out to other stores),
all in the same period (`dbt/models/semantic/metrics.yml`). Units received
count on the receipt date; units transferred out count on the delivery
date. Units sold are **gross**: returned units are destroyed, they never
go back on the shelf, so they are not subtracted.

## Options considered
- **Net received (chosen):** correct for one store and for the whole
  chain, because transfers cancel out when stores are added up.
- **Supplier deliveries only:** right for the chain, wrong for any store
  that receives transfers.
- **Suppliers plus transfers in:** right for one store, double counts
  transfers for the chain.
- **Units sold net of returns:** would treat destroyed stock as if it were
  never sold, and make heavy-return items look slow.

## Consequences
- A store that sends stock away has a higher sell-through than its sales
  alone suggest; that is correct, the stock left its shelf.
- The ratio can be above 1 in a month when a store sells down stock it
  received earlier. It only means something over weeks, not single days.
- Receipts and transfers are counted on different dates (receipt vs
  delivery), both visible in the marts.

## Worked example
Store NW014, August 2026, all in base units:
- Sold: 53,782.
- Received from suppliers: 48,852. Received by transfer: 140. Transferred
  out (delivered in August): 840.
- Net received = 48,852 + 140 - 840 = 48,152.
- `sell_through_rate` = 53,782 / 48,152 = **1.117**.

The rejected readings give 1.101 (suppliers only: 53,782 / 48,852) and
1.098 (suppliers plus transfers in: 53,782 / 48,992).
