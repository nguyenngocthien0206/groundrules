# DR-003: Cancelled transfers are not stock in transit

## Context
When a store ships stock to another store, the ERP keeps the units in the
sender's on-hand until the receiver confirms delivery. The units are
reserved, so the sender cannot sell them, but they still show on its
shelf. Available stock therefore has to subtract what is in transit.

The trap is in how the ERP records a cancellation. A cancelled transfer
(`CX`) keeps its ship date and never gets a received date, exactly like a
transfer still on the road (`TR`). A filter written as "shipped and not yet
received" counts the cancelled ones too and takes stock away from a store
that still has it. Two weekly reports were doing exactly that.

## Decision
`available_stock` subtracts outbound transfers whose status is
`in_transit` (ERP code `TR`) at the snapshot, counted against the sending
store (`outbound_in_transit_units`, `dbt/models/semantic/fct_transfers.yml`).
Cancelled (`CX`) and delivered (`DL`) transfers are never subtracted. The
status, not the dates, decides.

## Options considered
- **Status `in_transit` only (chosen):** matches what the ERP actually
  reserves. Needs the status code to be decoded, which staging does.
- **Shipped and not received:** what the old reports used. Simple, and
  wrong for every cancelled transfer.
- **Ignore transfers:** keeps the metric on one table, but double counts
  stock that is already on its way to another store.

## Consequences
- A cancellation gives the stock back to the sender at once in the
  metric, even before anyone updates the shelf count.
- The rule depends on the ERP's status codes. If the ERP adds a new code,
  the accepted-values test on `stg_erp__transfers` fails first.
- Receivers do not count stock in transit to them until it arrives; that
  is deliberate (they cannot sell it yet).

## Worked example
Snapshot 2026-09-15.

Store NW014, SKU 100002 "Paracetamol 325mg (Box 3x10)" (base unit: tablet)
has two batches, 568 and 1,758 tablets, both more than 800 days from
expiry, so 2,326 tablets are on long-dated stock. Transfer TRF01263 to
NW027 (30 tablets) shipped on 2026-09-13 and was cancelled on 2026-09-14:
it has a ship date and no received date.
- Correct: 2,326 - 0 = **2,326** tablets available.
- "Shipped and not received" filter: 2,326 - 30 = 2,296 (wrong by one box).

Store NW004, SKU 100010 "Ibuprofen 400mg (Box 3x10)" has 57 and 450
tablets on long-dated batches (507). Transfer TRF01279 (30 tablets) shipped
on 2026-09-14 and is still in transit.
- `available_stock` = 507 - 30 = **477** tablets.
